# 467 – Recycling & Waste Management

*Research date: 2026-05-02*

---

## 1. Problem Statement

Waste haulers, recycling processors, and municipal waste authorities manage complex daily operations: routing collection vehicles across hundreds of stops, tracking materials by stream and weight, meeting regulatory reporting requirements, and billing a mix of residential, commercial, and industrial customers. Without integrated software, route planning is inefficient, compliance data is assembled manually from paper records, and billing errors accumulate through mis-weighed loads and missed collections. A unified platform is needed to connect field operations, materials tracking, compliance, and revenue management.

## 3. Core Features

1. **Collection route planning and optimisation** – automatic generation of efficient collection routes considering vehicle capacity, stop frequency, time windows, and road restrictions.
2. **Dynamic route management** – real-time driver guidance via mobile app, missed-stop recording, and supervisor visibility of fleet progress against planned routes.
3. **Material stream tracking** – recording of material type, weight, and quality grade at each collection stop and at processing facilities, linked to customer and contract.
4. **Weighbridge and ticket management** – integration with vehicle scales, electronic weigh-ticket generation, and discrepancy alerts for over- or under-weight loads.
5. **Compliance reporting** – generation of statutory waste-transfer notes, manifests for hazardous material, and diversion-rate reports required by environmental regulators.
6. **Customer and contract management** – service agreements specifying collection frequency, container sizes, material streams, and contract rates for each customer.
7. **Billing and invoicing** – weight-based, volume-based, or flat-rate billing across residential, commercial, and roll-off customers, with exception charges for contaminated loads.
8. **Container asset tracking** – inventory of bins, skips, and roll-off containers with location, condition, and assignment to customer service points.
9. **Driver and vehicle management** – vehicle maintenance scheduling, pre-trip inspection records, driver licencing compliance, and incident reporting.
10. **Analytics and diversion dashboards** – landfill diversion rates, material recovery rates, route efficiency metrics, and customer-level sustainability reporting.

## 4. Technical Considerations

- **GPS and telematics integration** – real-time vehicle tracking from mixed telematics hardware (Geotab, Samsara, manufacturer systems) must feed into a unified operations view.
- **Mobile offline capability** – collection drivers in areas with poor cellular coverage need offline-capable apps that sync completed stops and exceptions when connectivity is restored.
- **Weighbridge API integration** – electronic scale systems use proprietary protocols (serial, TCP/IP) requiring hardware-specific adapters to generate accurate weigh tickets.
- **Regulatory complexity by jurisdiction** – waste-transfer note requirements, hazardous waste manifest rules, and diversion targets differ by state, county, and country; the compliance module must be configurable by jurisdiction.
- **AI-assisted route optimisation** – modern platforms use machine learning to improve route sequences over time based on actual collection times and traffic patterns.
- **Contamination tracking** – recording and reporting contamination incidents at the container level, including photo evidence, is increasingly required by material recovery facilities (MRFs).
- **Multi-material reconciliation** – materials collected may be weighed and invoiced differently depending on market commodity prices; the billing engine must support variable pricing per stream.
